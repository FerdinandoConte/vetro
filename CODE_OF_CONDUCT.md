# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) (CC BY 4.0). The summary below is binding; the linked text is the full reference.

## Our pledge
We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards
Encouraged: being kind and respectful, giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback, taking responsibility for mistakes, and focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable: sexualized language or imagery, trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political attacks, public or private harassment, publishing others' private information without permission, and other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities
Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and will take fair corrective action, including removing, editing or rejecting comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions, and temporarily or permanently banning anyone whose behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening or harmful.

## Scope
This applies in all project spaces (repository, issues, pull requests, discussions) and when an individual officially represents the project in public.

## Reporting
Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers through GitHub: open the repository's **Security** tab and choose **Report a vulnerability** (marking the report as a conduct issue), or contact a maintainer directly through their GitHub profile. All reports are reviewed promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy is respected.

## Enforcement guidelines
Maintainers follow the Contributor Covenant's four-step ladder: correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban. The full wording is in the version 2.1 text linked above.
